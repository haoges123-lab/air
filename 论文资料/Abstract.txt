# Abstract

With the continuous advancement of industrialization and urbanization in China, air pollution has become an increasingly severe problem, restricting sustainable economic and social development and affecting the health of the people. Air quality issues are not only related to the daily lives of hundreds of millions of people but also an important component of the country's ecological civilization construction. Timely and accurate air quality prediction is of great theoretical value and practical significance for urban environmental management, public health protection, and the formulation of scientific environmental policies.

Traditional air quality monitoring primarily relies on ground monitoring stations, which have limited coverage and cannot achieve large-scale, high-density real-time monitoring. Meanwhile, existing air quality prediction methods are mostly short-term predictions based on statistical models, lacking deep exploration of the spatio-temporal evolution patterns of pollutants, and both prediction accuracy and timeliness need improvement. In recent years, deep learning technology has made significant progress in the field of time series prediction, especially recurrent neural networks and their variants have shown unique advantages in processing sequential data, providing new technical approaches for air quality prediction.

This research focuses on the important issue of air quality prediction and designs and implements a model fusion-based Chinese air quality prediction system. Using Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory networks (Bi-LSTM) as the core, combined with province embedding technology and multi-scale time feature fusion strategies, this system constructs a deep learning prediction model capable of capturing the spatio-temporal evolution patterns of air pollutants.

In terms of model design, this research adopts a bidirectional LSTM network structure that can simultaneously utilize past and future directional context information from historical data, effectively extracting long-term dependencies in time series. The model innovatively introduces a province embedding layer, encoding geographic spatial information into low-dimensional vectors, enabling the model to learn differences in air quality patterns across different regions. At the same time, the model integrates periodic time features (annual, weekly, monthly cycles) and seasonal features, enhancing the model's ability to capture the periodic variation patterns of air pollution.

In terms of model fusion, this research proposes an ensemble prediction strategy based on prediction uncertainty weighting. Aiming at the characteristics of different pollutant prediction difficulties, the model uses neural networks for major pollutants such as SO₂, NO₂, CO, and O₃, while using naive baseline methods (using recent historical values) for indicators such as PM2.5, PM10, and AQI, improving system robustness while ensuring prediction accuracy.

In terms of system implementation, this research constructs a complete front-end and back-end system architecture. The back-end adopts the FastAPI framework to provide RESTful API services, supporting functions such as real-time city data query, historical data acquisition, and air quality prediction. The front-end adopts ECharts visualization technology, implementing modules including national air quality map visualization, city air quality details viewing, 24-hour trend analysis, historical data query, and AI intelligent prediction, providing the public with intuitive and convenient air quality information services.

Experimental results show that the bidirectional LSTM prediction model designed in this research achieves good prediction effects on air quality prediction tasks across Chinese provinces. Through comparative analysis with naive baseline models, the effectiveness and superiority of deep learning methods in the field of air quality prediction are verified. The system can provide decision support for environmental management departments and health advice for public travel, having important application value.

**Keywords**: Air Quality Prediction; Bidirectional Long Short-Term Memory Network; Model Fusion; Time Series Analysis; Deep Learning
